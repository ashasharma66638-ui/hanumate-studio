Our Story

About Hanumate Studio

Hanumate Studio is an independent creative studio based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, focused on developing original animated stories, manga, anime-inspired projects, character designs and cinematic visual experiences.

Our work explores the meeting point between Indian culture, mythology, modern imagination and the visual language of animation.
